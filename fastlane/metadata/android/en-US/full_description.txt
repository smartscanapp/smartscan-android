Turn your images and videos into a personal knowledge base. Search, organise, and rediscover the media you've saved, with automatic collections and concepts that help you find information you might otherwise forget.

<b>Search</b>

<ul>
<li>Find images and videos using text or image search</li>
<li>Search by tags or tag + text queries</li>
<li>Search from other apps using share or intent</li>
<li>Paste images directly into the search bar</li>
</ul>

<b>Automatic Collections</b>

<ul>
<li>Automatically group visually similar media into collections</li>
<li>Browse related photos and videos without manually organising them</li>
</ul>

<b>Concepts</b>

<ul>
<li>Organise media by meaning, not just appearance</li>
<li>Create concepts around topics or ideas and automatically find matching media using descriptions</li>
<li>Generate image descriptions automatically or add and edit descriptions yourself</li>
</ul>

<b>Tagging</b>

<ul>
<li>Add tags to your media</li>
<li>Use tag autocomplete when searching or tagging</li>
</ul>

<b>Duplicate Management</b>

<ul>
<li>Detect duplicate media</li>
<li>Filter your library to include, exclude, or show only duplicates</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

<ul>
<li>SmartScan is offline-first</li>
<li>Your media, tags, collections, concepts, and generated descriptions are stored and processed on your device</li>
<li>Generating image descriptions optionally uses cloud processing and requires an internet connection</li>
</ul>
